# Image-role and independent-source formula diagnostics

Experiment date: October 3, 2026. Report assembled October 4, 2026. All claims below are development evidence from fixed, untrained Qwen2-VL-2B inference; they do not establish a benchmark improvement or a novel method.

## Main decision

Do not scale the current prompt into SFT/GRPO on the strength of synthetic perturbation repairs. On the 16-source native Nougat-LaTeX development set, none of the three repair conditions fixes an initially incorrect output under the frozen same-renderer raster proxy, while all three damage initially matching outputs. Output-wrapper normalization does not remove this result. Proposal quality, acceptance quality, and correct-input preservation need separate evaluation.

## Repeated synthetic image-role diagnostic

All 20 October 2 cases were retained: eight incorrect inputs and twelve correct/equivalent inputs, derived from four templates and eight source-image directions. The six conditions produced 120 calls. Source-only and original source-first each reproduced their previous 20 final strings exactly.

| Evidence condition | Errors fixed / 8 | Good regressions / 12 | Full-set raster matches / 20 | Mean generation seconds | Mean input tokens |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Unchanged | 0 | 0 | 12 | 0 | 0 |
| Source only | 7 | 0 | 19 | 2.51 | 251.95 |
| Source first | 2 | 0 | 14 | 3.48 | 404.70 |
| Source last | 5 | 0 | 17 | 3.51 | 404.70 |
| Interleaved labels, source first | 1 | 0 | 13 | 3.51 | 413.70 |
| Interleaved labels, source last | 5 | 0 | 17 | 3.55 | 413.70 |
| Duplicate source | 7 | 0 | 19 | 3.50 | 404.70 |

Reversing image order with the corresponding role-index correction changes repair behavior. Labels do not consistently improve it. A duplicate source does not reproduce the collapse, so the result cannot be explained solely by the presence of a second image. This measures behavior rather than internal attention, and the tiny reused template set is not an independent test. There is one render rollback in the labeled-source-last condition. See [portable evidence](../../experiments/artifacts/image-role-20261003/README.md).

## Independently sourced formulas

The frozen 16-image selection uses the official UniMER archive, numeric-filename annotation mapping, and deterministic SHA-256 ordering. Eight SPE and eight SCE images were selected before repair inference, with MathText-supported labels of at most 200 characters and a visible perturbation. Full Tectonic rendering became available afterward; the sample selection was not changed. Source/label eligibility creates selection bias, and pretraining overlap cannot be excluded. Every inspected source is now development data.

The controlled condition pairs each source with its released reference and a one-symbol perturbation: 32 inputs, 16 initially matching and 16 initially nonmatching. The separate native condition uses all 16 outputs of `Norm/nougat-latex-base` with its author's custom processor, five beams and max length 800. No native output was selected by correctness or rendering success. Ten native outputs match the reference raster and six do not. One native output fails rendering and remains in all denominators; the two-image methods preserve that baseline without a model call. Its source-only method still runs.

| Set and method | Errors fixed | Initially matching regressions | Final raster matches | Calls | Candidate render failures | Mean generation seconds per case |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Controlled, unchanged | 0/16 | 0/16 | 16/32 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Controlled, source only | 10/16 | 3/16 | 23/32 | 32 | 7 | 5.71 |
| Controlled, source first | 1/16 | 3/16 | 14/32 | 32 | 9 | 7.62 |
| Controlled, source last | 3/16 | 1/16 | 18/32 | 32 | 11 | 8.10 |
| Native, unchanged | 0/6 | 0/10 | 10/16 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Native, source only | 0/6 | 2/10 | 8/16 | 16 | 6 | 6.45 |
| Native, source first | 0/6 | 3/10 | 7/16 | 15 | 5 | 7.58 |
| Native, source last | 0/6 | 1/10 | 9/16 | 15 | 4 | 7.54 |

The repaired controlled distribution differs from native OCR errors. Do not interpret the former as transfer gains. Correctly matching inputs can be replaced by coordinate tuples such as `(10,10),(988,988)`, which are syntactically renderable but unrelated to the formula. Other responses copy the wrong initial symbol or include prompt prose as formula content. Zero responses hit the 128-token cap in either real-image repair batch. Timing is CPU bfloat16 model generation only, excludes rendering/queueing, and is not a GPU or end-to-end deployment latency claim. The controlled/native jobs partly overlapped, so speed comparisons are descriptive.

## Output-adapter controls, post hoc

No model call or prompt is changed. Two fixed replay policies inspect generated output before labels are loaded: normalize only a whole display environment (`equation`/`displaymath` to its body, `align`/`gather` to math-safe equivalents), or require the explicitly requested `<latex>` wrapper before applying that normalization. These are exploratory controls designed after inspecting failures.

| Normalized whole-environment replay | Errors fixed | Good regressions | Final raster matches |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Controlled, source only | 11/16 | 4/16 | 23/32 |
| Controlled, source first | 2/16 | 4/16 | 14/32 |
| Controlled, source last | 3/16 | 3/16 | 16/32 |
| Native, source only | 0/6 | 2/10 | 8/16 |
| Native, source first | 0/6 | 3/10 | 7/16 |
| Native, source last | 0/6 | 1/10 | 9/16 |

Normalization removes most compilation failures but can accept additional harmful edits. All 32 source-only controlled responses, 31 source-first responses, and 30 source-last responses omit the requested wrapper. Requiring that wrapper fixes no controlled errors and preserves the original 16/32 matches. On native data the strict gate also fixes no errors; source-first still regresses one good input, while source-only/source-last preserve the original count. A format contract alone does not certify image fidelity.

## Agreement controls, post hoc

Compare exact rasters of the fixed final proposals, without reading references. Reject disagreement or failed renders, preserve the initial output, and charge every proposal call used by the gate. Accept the named proposal when the required arms agree. Comparing post-rollback outputs can create agreement simply because both arms fell back to the same initial prediction; agreement count is therefore distinct from accepted edits.

| Gate | Controlled fixes / 16 | Controlled regressions / 16 | Native fixes / 6 | Native regressions / 10 | Controlled accepted edits | Native accepted edits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Source first + source last, accept source last | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Source only + source last, accept source only | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 3 |
| All three, accept source only | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3 |

Two image orders agree on the same wrong coordinates for a matching native input. The all-three gate buys preservation with no controlled repairs; the source-only/source-last gate retains only two controlled repairs and no native fixes. None establishes a useful native quality/cost frontier. Gates were examined on development data after inference; choosing the best one here would require a new, untouched evaluation set and matched-cost comparators.

## Visual and annotation audit

All 48 controlled/native input rows were inspected using source/reference/initial/three-final contact sheets. These sheets are private generated evidence, because benchmark source images are not republished in the artifact package. The raw labels and published exact-raster scores remain frozen.

The native mg-to-math-symbol error, AC-subscript placement, trailing equation punctuation, and unsupported `permille` output are distinct content/structure/rendering failures. One crop visibly has a short mark above Pc missing from the released reference, and a second crop appears to have trailing punctuation beyond its reference. These concerns are recorded separately in [annotation_audit.jsonl](../../experiments/artifacts/unimer-development-20261003/annotation_audit.jsonl); they have not been used to relabel or improve results. Exact-raster mismatch also includes style and spacing differences. Neither a mismatch nor a match is a general visual-correctness certificate.

## Reproducibility and next decision

The [real-image evidence package](../../experiments/artifacts/unimer-development-20261003/README.md) preserves source IDs/hashes, all selection exclusions, reference-free inputs, pinned models/processors/code, fixed outputs, all-case evaluation, and post-hoc controls. No source images or weights are committed. Scores replay from predictions and offline labels without a GPU; source-image reconstruction requires the pinned official archive.

The next experiment must test native error correction and preservation under improved source grounding, with an image-only recognition control to detect anchoring by the initial candidate. Table regions remain required: cell content, merged spans, row/column structure, and TEDS/TEDS-S need a separate controlled and native-output pilot. Only after proposal quality is measurable should we compare a verifier, distillation/SFT, and RL at a fixed budget. Prior art already covers many components; [the novelty check](PRIOR_ART_20261003.md) does not establish a new contribution.
